# 1.7.14

## Roundtable: build your lineup by talking to the moderator

Creating a roundtable is now a conversation, not a form. Tap "+" in the Roundtable Center and you land in a casting chat:

- The moderator proposes a lineup of role cards (persona, MBTI, and why each one is there) and explains its thinking.
- You refine it by chatting — "swap the least relevant one", "add someone who pushes back", "make it more UX-focused" — and the lineup updates.
- When you're happy, tap "确认开议 / Start" and the roundtable launches straight into the live discussion.

The old form-based setup is still available as "Manual setup" from the casting screen and the Roundtable Center menu.

## Notes

- The roundtable discussion and the casting moderator now run on a real LLM (server-side), so proposals and debate are genuinely reasoned rather than canned.
- Client change only here; the live discussion screen, OpenCode chat, and credential-safety route checks are unchanged.
