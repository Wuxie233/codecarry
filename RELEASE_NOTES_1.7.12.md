# 1.7.12

## Roundtable: watch the live discussion and join in

Tapping a roundtable in the Roundtable Center now opens the **live discussion** instead of a static summary. You land in the conversation view where you can:

- Watch each persona take turns in real time, see them build on and push back against each other, and read the moderator's per-round synthesis.
- **Speak to the whole table**: your message is broadcast to every agent, so you can add information or steer the discussion at any time.
- Use the steering controls (@mention a persona, continue/stop the round, go deeper on a point, introduce a new persona).

"Resume" on a paused roundtable now also takes you straight into the live view.

The exported **summary** (transcript, knowledge network, open questions) is still available from the roundtable card's "..." menu via "View summary".

## Notes

- Navigation-only change for the roundtable feature; the live discussion screen and the OpenCode session flow are unchanged.
- The Pi server bearer token is never placed in the roundtable browse routes (guarded by tests).
