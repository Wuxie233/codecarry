# OC Remote v1.6.14 — Release Notes

## Session list & subagent UX

- **Subagent visibility optimization**: the Active Subagents area now defaults to showing only subagents that are currently working (`Busy` / `Retry`).
- **Optional history toggle**: Settings now includes a switch to also show historical idle subagents when you want the fuller history view.
- **Persistent preference**: the toggle is saved globally and survives app restarts.
